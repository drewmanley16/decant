Scan

Point at a label. That's it.

Decant reads the label and finds the wine, the producer, the region and the vintage. Rate it in a tap, add a few words, and it's in your diary for good.

  • No typing: it reads the label for you
  • Pick the right vintage in one tap
  • Private notes stay private

Cellar

Your cellar, on real shelves.

Every bottle you own, drawn with its own label and sorted by style. Pick one up, turn it around, and open it when the time is right.

  • Know what you have and what you paid
  • Open a bottle and log it in one step
  • Your whole wine history in one place

Friends

Drink with your people.

See what friends are opening, cheer the good ones, and bookmark anything you want to try. Recommendations follow the grapes, regions and styles you love.

  • A feed of your friends' tastings
  • One tap to save a wine to your wishlist
  • You choose who sees each review

Your palate

Learn what you actually like.

Tastings, countries, regions, average rating and month-by-month history. The more you log, the better Decant knows your taste.
