Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Decant helps you scan, rate, collect and share wine. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you choose Sign in with Apple and share it. Apple's "Hide My Email" is supported.
  • Profile: username, display name, optional bio and profile photo, privacy settings and favorite wine styles.
  • Content you create: ratings, reviews, private tasting notes, tasting photos, dates, locations or occasions you type in, comments, likes, follows, wishlist, cellar inventory and arrangement, and collections.
  • Label scans: photos of wine labels you scan and the text read from them.
  • Safety reports: reports you file and accounts you block.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, health data, or advertising identifiers. Camera and photo-library access are used only when you scan a label or attach a photo.

How we use it

  • To run the app: identify wines, store your diary and cellar, show your content to the people you choose, and send in-app notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports and enforce our Community Guidelines.
  • To improve wine identification: recognized labels are cached so the same label is identified faster next time.

Who can see your content

You choose the visibility of each review: Public, Friends (people you follow who follow you back), or Only me. Private tasting notes, your cellar, wishlist and scan history are visible only to you. A private profile hides your content from anyone you haven't approved. Your username, display name and profile photo are visible to other signed-in users so friends can find you.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
  • OpenAI reads label photos you scan to identify the wine. Label images and the text read from them are sent to OpenAI for this purpose only. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete individual tastings at any time, or delete your entire account in Profile → Settings → Delete account. That permanently removes your profile, diary, reviews, comments, photos, cellar, scans and reports. Wine catalog entries you helped create (a wine's name, producer and vintage) may remain, without any link to you.

Age

Decant is about alcohol and is only for adults of legal drinking age in their country. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, contact us at drewmanley16@gmail.com.

Changes

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and let you know in the app.
