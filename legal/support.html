Common questions

A label wasn't recognized. Fill the frame with the front label in good light, then try again. You can always tap Manual to add the wine yourself.

The app matched the wrong vintage. On the scan result, choose "Use the [year] vintage", or pick the correct wine from "Other possibilities".

How do I report or block someone? Tap "…" on any review, comment or profile. Manage blocked accounts in Profile → Settings → Blocked accounts.

How do I make my reviews private? Choose Friends or Only me when you log a tasting, or set a default in Settings. You can also make your whole profile private.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes all your data.
